# Code of Conduct

Drakeshard Studios is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and constructive environment across our repositories and community spaces.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and in good faith.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and perspectives.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and confidential information.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for contributors and players.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse
- Personal insults or sustained hostile behavior
- Sexualized or otherwise inappropriate conduct
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Spam, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith participation
- Retaliation against someone who raises a conduct concern

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, remove, or reject contributions and communications that violate this policy. Depending on severity or repetition, maintainers may issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant from Drakeshard-managed spaces.

Moderation decisions should be based on behavior and project safety rather than disagreement over technical choices.

## Reporting

If a conduct concern involves a public thread, you may report the relevant content using GitHub's reporting features where available.

For sensitive matters, contact the maintainers privately through an established Drakeshard contact channel. A dedicated contact address will be added when one is available.

Reports will be handled with discretion to the extent reasonably possible.
